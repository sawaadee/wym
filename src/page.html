        • Encrypted in the browser with AES-128-GCM. The key is only in the link, after the #, which browsers never send to a server. After a link is opened, this page removes the key from the address bar.
        • Every byte between sender and recipient goes through the Nym mixnet. The sender answers through single-use reply blocks and never learns the recipient's Nym address.
        • A mixnet trades speed for privacy. Expect 15 to 25 seconds of setup before files start moving, then roughly 15 to 25 KB per second.
        • Network mode, at the top: Balanced sends more steady cover traffic and is somewhat faster, using about 1.4 GB of data an hour while connected. Maximum privacy uses Nym's default settings and about 0.8 GB an hour. The page disconnects after 5 minutes without activity.
